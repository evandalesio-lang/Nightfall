NIGHTFALL

Your personal horror story made by your choices

This app talks to a small backend (for accounts and to keep the story-writing API key private) that hasn't been configured in this file. Whoever is setting this up needs to open the HTML file in a text editor, find the SUPABASE_URL and SUPABASE_ANON_KEY constants near the top of the <script> tag, and fill them in with values from their Supabase project. See the setup guide that came with this file for the full walkthrough.

No email needed — just pick a username and password. Your friends each get their own account and their own saved stories.
